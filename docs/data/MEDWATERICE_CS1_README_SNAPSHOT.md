# METHODOLOGICAL INFORMATION

## Description of methods used for collection/generation of data:  

In the experimental platform a soil survey was conducted prior to the 2019 agricultural season through an Electro-Magnetic Induction (EMI) sensor and the collection of soil samples at different soil depths subsequently analysed for the determination of a series of physico-chemical parameters. One out of the two plots irrigated with each specific irrigation strategy was instrumented with water inflow and outflow metres, a set of piezometers, tensiometers, water tubes (this last device only in the AWD plots, for the management of the intermittent irrigation). In the same three plots, periodic measurements of crop biometric parameters (LAI, crop height, crop rooting depth) were performed during the agricultural seasons 2019 and 2020. Nutrients (N, P, K) and two widely used pesticides (Sirtaki – active ingredient (a.i.) Clomazone; Tripion E – active ingredient MCPA) were periodically monitored in the irrigation water (inflow and outflow), groundwater, and soil solution collected by porous cups installed at two soil depths (20 and 70 cm, above and below the plough pan) in the three plots. For all the plots in the platform, rice grain yields, rice grain and straw nitrogen uptake, and arsenic and cadmium analysis of rice grain were performed). Additionally, agro-climatic data (air temperature, air humidity, rainfall, wind velocity and direction) were measured at the agro-metrological station installed in the experimental farm. 



# DATA-SPECIFIC INFORMATION FOR and Methods for porcessing data: [All datasets]

Data collected in each pilot farm in the agricultural seasons were organized in Excel format files. Image files (JPG, GIF, SVG, PNG, TIFF) illustrating the layout of the experimental activities in each pilot farm, and additional PDF files presenting the rice varieties cropped during the three experimental seasons were moreover prepared. Data series refer to a period of about 150 days (i.e. average length of the rice crop cycle in the Mediterranean basin). Average data volume produced for each experimental year for each CS is about 5 MB.
In the MEDWATERICE project, NO sensible data have been collected during the research. Data collected are compliant with GDPR. NO human biological samples have been collected in the research. NO children have been involved in the research.

Each Excel file contains 13 sheets. The first sheet, titled "General", provides details such as: type of data, variable, units, whether the variable is measured (M) or estimated (E), the time step of measurement/estimation (e.g., every 10 minutes, hourly, daily, seasonal, etc.), the number of measurement/estimation points for the experimental farm, the measuring device or estimation method, and the data format (Excel)."
