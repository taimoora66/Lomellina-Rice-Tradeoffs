# GO / NO-GO Rules

**Protocol version:** 1.0  
**Frozen:** 2026-08-29

These gates prevent the project from progressing on assumptions that
have not been demonstrated.

## G1 — Publication Novelty

Does the fixed-composition configuration and synchronization research
gap survive the systematic competitor search?

GO:
A defensible and sufficiently distinct contribution remains.

NO-GO:
A prior study already answers substantially the same question at a
comparable scale and system.

Action on failure:
Reframe or abandon the publication hypothesis.

## G2 — Open-Data EFS Feasibility

Can the EFS report be completed reproducibly using available data and
evidence?

GO:
Core hydroperiod and ecosystem-service analyses are feasible.

NO-GO:
Core variables cannot be reproduced or interpreted reliably.

Action on failure:
Reduce or redesign the quantitative EFS component.

## G3 — MEDWATERICE Usability

Can MEDWATERICE experimental data and management definitions be
interpreted correctly and reproduced?

GO:
Variables, treatments and units are sufficiently documented.

NO-GO:
Critical definitions or data structures remain ambiguous.

Action on failure:
Use verified published results rather than unsupported reconstruction.

## G4 — Sentinel Validation

Does independent Sentinel validation support the intended inference?

GO:
Performance is adequate for the specific hydroperiod/inundation outcome.

NO-GO:
Performance is inadequate or validation is not independent.

Action on failure:
Restrict or remove Sentinel-based claims.

## G5 — Hydrological Platform

Can an appropriate district-scale hydrological platform be accessed,
understood, reproduced and validated?

GO:
Model structure and inputs are sufficiently transparent and reproducible.

NO-GO:
Required platform or calibration cannot be reproduced.

Action on failure:
Stop Publication Path P1.

## G6 — Operational Hydrological Data

Are diversion, network, groundwater and return-flow processes
sufficiently constrained?

GO:
The system can support defensible district-level inference.

NO-GO:
Key operational components remain unconstrained.

Action on failure:
Do not make district water-saving or configuration claims.

## G7 — Configuration Signal

Does the fixed-composition configuration effect exceed relevant
observational and model uncertainty?

GO:
Effect is practically and statistically meaningful.

NO-GO:
Effect is negligible or indistinguishable from uncertainty.

Action on failure:
STOP OPTIMIZATION.

This is a hard stopping rule.

## G8 — Biological Evidence

Are suitable biological observations available?

GO:
Biological analysis may be considered.

NO-GO:
No adequate biological observations exist.

Action on failure:
Use habitat-availability indicators only.

## G9 — GHG Modelling

Are sufficiently local water, soil, fertilizer, residue and flux data
available for calibrated process modelling?

GO:
Detailed GHG modelling may be evaluated.

NO-GO:
Inputs or calibration are inadequate.

Action on failure:
Use aggregate empirical evidence and uncertainty ranges only.

## G10 — Final Novelty Audit

Immediately before manuscript submission, repeat the competitor and
literature search.

GO:
The contribution remains distinct.

NO-GO:
New work has already closed the gap.

Action on failure:
Revise or abandon the novelty claim before submission.
