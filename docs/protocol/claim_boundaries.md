# Claim Boundaries

**Protocol version:** 1.0  
**Frozen:** 2026-08-29

This file defines claims that the project must not make without new,
specific supporting evidence.

## Prohibited Claims

Do not state that:

1. AWD is universally environmentally superior.
2. Sentinel-1 directly identifies AWD management.
3. Sentinel-1 continuously observes flooded days.
4. Reduced field irrigation automatically equals district water saving.
5. Reduced field irrigation automatically equals basin water saving.
6. Hydroperiod is equivalent to biodiversity.
7. Flooded area is equivalent to biodiversity.
8. Generic literature coefficients precisely predict parcel methane.
9. Generic literature coefficients precisely predict parcel nitrous oxide.
10. Generic literature coefficients precisely predict parcel rice yield.
11. Pixels constitute independent experimental replicates.
12. Modelled scenarios automatically establish causal farmer effects.
13. A Pareto frontier identifies a socially optimal policy.
14. Combining multiple ecosystem-service outcomes is itself a novel contribution.
15. Application to Lomellina is itself sufficient novelty.
16. Model-estimated quantities are observations.
17. District-level results automatically generalize to Italy or Europe.

## Required Inference Language

Results should be described according to their evidential status.

### Observed

Directly measured or obtained from validated observational datasets.

### Descriptive

A summary of observed patterns.

### Associated

A statistical relationship that does not establish causality.

### Literature-supported

An interpretation supported by external empirical literature.

### Model-estimated

A quantity generated through a model.

### Counterfactual

A hypothetical scenario generated under specified assumptions.

### Causal

Use only where the study design actually supports causal inference.

## Ecology Boundary

Without direct biological observations, permitted terminology includes:

- wetland-habitat availability;
- hydroperiod;
- flooded-area continuity;
- landscape water connectivity;
- distance to permanent wetlands;
- refuge availability.

Do not infer:

- biodiversity increase or decline;
- species richness;
- amphibian abundance;
- bird abundance;
- ecological condition

without appropriate biological evidence.

## Greenhouse-Gas Boundary

For the EFS report, literature-supported ranges and local experimental
evidence may be used.

Do not construct parcel-level CH4 or N2O estimates from generic
literature multipliers and present them as observed or locally validated
emissions.

## Production Boundary

Yield evidence should be based on suitable experimental or observational
sources.

For the publication experiment, production should initially act as a
feasibility or non-inferiority constraint rather than as a falsely
precise parcel-level objective.
