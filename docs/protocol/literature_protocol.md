# Literature Review Protocol

**Protocol version:** 1.0  
**Status:** TO BE COMPLETED DURING PHASE 2A

## Purpose

The literature review serves two separate functions:

1. establish the evidence base for the EFS report;
2. independently test whether the publication novelty claim survives.

## Evidence Streams

L1 — Hydrology and field-to-district scaling

L2 — Methane, nitrous oxide and greenhouse-gas trade-offs

L3 — Yield, irrigation requirement and water productivity

L4 — Ecology, hydroperiod and wetland-habitat availability

L5 — Remote sensing, spatial allocation and optimization

## Geographic Classification

Studies will be classified as:

1. Lomellina / San Giorgio
2. Lombardy / Piedmont / Po Valley
3. Italy
4. Mediterranean Europe
5. European temperate rice systems
6. international / global evidence

## Required Extraction Fields

Each included source should record:

- title;
- authors;
- year;
- DOI;
- journal or source;
- geography;
- study years;
- scale;
- experimental unit;
- management definition;
- comparator;
- hydrological variables;
- GHG variables;
- production variables;
- biological variables;
- remote-sensing method;
- validation method;
- statistical method;
- spatial dependence treatment;
- temporal dependence treatment;
- uncertainty;
- quantitative result;
- transferability;
- data availability;
- limitations;
- potential bias;
- relevance to EFS;
- relevance to publication novelty.

## Novelty Rule

A publication gap is not established merely because multiple topics have
not previously been combined.

The strongest competing paper must be identified explicitly.

The search must actively attempt to falsify the proposed novelty claim.
