# Evidence Chronology and Claim Classification

Status: FROZEN RESEARCH CHRONOLOGY
Date: 2026-08-31

## Purpose

This document records the chronological evidentiary status of the
RiceFloodIT-compatible flooding and ARPA groundwater publication analysis.

Its purpose is to prevent retrospective relabeling of exploratory,
historically informed, held-out, or subsequent-extension analyses.

Scientific decisions must be interpreted according to what information was
available when each decision was made.

No later result may cause an earlier exploratory analysis to be described as
confirmatory or an outcome-informed extension to be described as an untouched
independent replication.

---

## 1. Historical exploratory phase: 2008-2021

### Information available

The historical RiceFloodIT and ARPA groundwater data were explored across
multiple analytical choices, including alternative groundwater timing,
weather formulations, exposure supports, transformations, falsification
tests, nonlinear specifications, and related model structures.

An August-groundwater / 10-km flooding association emerged during this
exploratory work.

### Evidentiary status

EXPLORATORY / HYPOTHESIS-GENERATING.

The historical August / 10-km signal was not generated by a single
prespecified hypothesis test.

The recovered exploratory analysis therefore cannot be treated as
confirmatory evidence.

### Consequence

The selected exploratory coefficient and nominal p-value must remain
qualified by the documented researcher degrees of freedom and multiplicity
record.

---

## 2. Historical data reconstruction

### Decision

A production reconstruction of the 2008-2021 groundwater publication data
foundation was developed independently of the recovered exploratory
intermediate files.

Groundwater timing variables were reconstructed explicitly from raw
observation dates.

### Information already known

The broad historical August / 10-km association was already known before this
production reconstruction was completed.

### Evidentiary status

REPRODUCIBILITY / DATA-FOUNDATION WORK.

Successful computational reconstruction strengthens provenance and data
quality but does not convert the historical exploratory association into a
confirmatory finding.

---

## 3. Clean historical publication-model freeze

### Decision

A clean historical specification was frozen before its own coefficient was
inspected:

`gw_aug_mean_m ~ ff_10_anom + gw_pre_last_janfeb_m + P_A8 + T_A8 + C(station) + C(year)`

Spatial-inference rules and sensitivity cutoffs were also recorded before the
clean-model result was revealed.

### Information already known

The historical exploratory work had already identified an August / 10-km
relationship before this clean model was specified.

### Evidentiary status

FROZEN HISTORICAL RE-ANALYSIS, BUT HISTORICALLY INFORMED.

The clean model was outcome-blind with respect to its own exact coefficient
after specification freeze, but its scientific architecture existed within a
research program already informed by prior historical exploration.

It is therefore not an independent confirmation of the historical discovery.

---

## 4. Historical multiplicity audit

### Finding

At least nine directly comparable historical flooding-coefficient tests were
recovered from the exploratory search history.

Three had nominal p-values below 0.05, but none survived Bonferroni, Holm, or
Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment across this minimum nine-test family.

The nine-test family is a documented lower bound rather than the complete
historical specification-search universe.

### Evidentiary consequence

The original exploratory historical association is not treated as
multiplicity-robust confirmatory evidence.

The clean historical re-analysis remains scientifically informative but must
be interpreted in the context of this prior selection history.

---

## 5. Satellite measurement-bridge development

### Scientific separation

The post-2021 flooding reconstruction was developed as a measurement exercise
against historical RiceFloodIT observations.

Post-2021 groundwater outcomes were prohibited from influencing:

- MODIS geometry;
- QA treatment;
- spectral-index construction;
- spatial aggregation;
- temporal aggregation;
- bridge model form;
- bridge coefficients;
- historical validation decisions.

### Development history

An initial linear bridge was evaluated and rejected because it produced
physically invalid negative flooding-fraction predictions.

Those validation years were then considered used.

A bounded fractional-logit bridge was subsequently specified before opening
new historical confirmation periods.

The final fixed bridge was independently checked on historical years outside
its development period.

### Evidentiary status

INDEPENDENT MEASUREMENT VALIDATION WITH DOCUMENTED MODEL DEVELOPMENT.

The final reconstruction is described as a
RiceFloodIT-compatible reconstructed flooding signal.

It is not described as an exact reconstruction of the undocumented original
RiceFloodIT algorithm or as direct irrigation volume.

## 6. Frozen flooding anomaly and spatial exposure

Before post-2021 groundwater association results were inspected, the
following were frozen:

- reconstructed flooding baseline: cell-specific 2010-2021 mean;
- annual anomaly relative to that baseline;
- 10-km well support;
- unweighted arithmetic mean across RiceFloodIT-compatible cell centers;
- fixed historical spatial geometry.

These definitions were subsequently extended through 2025 without
groundwater-outcome tuning.

### Evidentiary status

OUTCOME-INDEPENDENT POST-2021 EXPOSURE CONSTRUCTION.

The historical origin of the 10-km radius remains acknowledged: 10 km emerged
from earlier exploratory historical work and was subsequently frozen for the
post-2021 analysis.

## 7. Original 2022-2023 held-out groundwater confirmation

### Freeze

Before inspecting the post-2021 flooding-groundwater relationship, the
following were frozen:

- 13 repeated ISS wells;
- 2022-2023 contrast;
- `gw_aug_nearest_aug23_m` outcome;
- `gw_pre_last_janfeb_m` antecedent groundwater;
- 10-km frozen flooding anomaly;
- first-difference OLS;
- HC3 inference;
- weather robustness specifications;
- influence diagnostics.

### Evidentiary status

GENUINE HELD-OUT CONFIRMATION / FALSIFICATION EXERCISE.

This is the strongest outcome-independent association test in the project
chronology.

### Result

The 2022-2023 held-out analysis did not corroborate the historical positive
association.

The estimate was highly imprecise, its confidence interval included large
effects in both directions, and leave-one-well-out coefficients did not retain
a common sign.

### Consequence

This result remains part of the permanent evidence record.

It cannot be removed, replaced, or retrospectively reclassified if later
years produce a different result.

---

## 8. Decision to extend groundwater and covariate data beyond 2023

### Information already known

The 2022-2023 held-out groundwater result had already been revealed before the
scientific interpretation of a longer post-2021 analysis was developed.

### Action

Additional openly available groundwater and meteorological observations were
extended through 2024 and subsequently 2025.

The already frozen flooding measurement bridge and anomaly construction were
also applied through 2025.

### Evidentiary status

SUBSEQUENT DATA EXTENSION AFTER THE ORIGINAL HELD-OUT RESULT WAS KNOWN.

The motivation for evaluating a longer post-2021 period is therefore informed
by the earlier held-out result.

The 2024-2025 extension must not be described as an untouched independent
confirmation conceived before the 2022-2023 result.

The extended production pipelines were required to reproduce previously

STRONG COMPUTATIONAL CONTINUITY EVIDENCE.

These checks demonstrate that addition of 2024-2025 did not silently redefine
the previously frozen 2022-2023 analysis.

They do not make the later scientific question independent of the earlier
results.

## 10. Integrated 2022-2025 eligibility audit

Before fitting any 2022-2025 flooding-groundwater association model, the
integrated availability structure was audited.

Eligibility required:

- `gw_aug_nearest_aug23_m`;
- `gw_pre_last_janfeb_m`;
- valid frozen 10-km FF10 anomaly;
- positive 10-km flooding geometry;
- `P_A8`;
- `T_A8`.

Observed eligible counts:

- 2022: 17 wells;
- 2023: 13 wells;
- 2024: 15 wells;
- 2025: 17 wells.

Across 2022-2025:

- 12 wells are eligible in all four years;
- 14 wells are eligible in at least three years;
- 18 wells are eligible in at least two years.

Flooding availability removed zero groundwater-complete station-years.

Weather availability removed zero groundwater-plus-flooding-complete
station-years.

The generalized eligibility pipeline exactly reproduced the 13 station IDs
used by the original frozen 2022-2023 confirmation.

### Evidentiary status

PRE-MODEL AVAILABILITY AND SAMPLE-STRUCTURE INFORMATION.

These availability patterns are known before any 2022-2025 association
coefficient is fitted.

Future sample and estimator decisions may therefore legitimately consider
data completeness and panel structure, but they must be described as
pre-analysis decisions made with availability information already known.

## 11. Status of the 2022-2025 association analysis

At the time of this chronology freeze:

- no 2022-2025 flooding-groundwater association coefficient has been fitted;
- no 2022-2025 association sign has been inspected;
- no 2022-2025 p-value or confidence interval has been inspected;
- no primary 2022-2025 model has been selected according to an observed
  coefficient.

### Correct evidentiary description

The forthcoming 2022-2025 analysis is a:

PRESPECIFIED SUBSEQUENT EXTENSION CONDITIONAL ON PRIOR KNOWLEDGE.

It is not:

- a pristine independent replication;
- a preregistration made before the original held-out result;
- a replacement for the 2022-2023 confirmation;
- a causal experiment.

Its variables, sample hierarchy, estimand, estimator, inference rule, and
robustness hierarchy will be frozen before its own association coefficient is
revealed.

## 12. Claim boundaries

The publication may evaluate:

- historical association;
- historical selection and multiplicity;
- independent measurement reconstruction;
- failure or success of temporal corroboration;
- temporal stability or instability;
- uncertainty and transport of observational associations.

The publication must not claim:

- randomized or quasi-random causal identification;
- that rice flooding has been proven to recharge or deplete groundwater;
- that the reconstructed flooding metric is direct irrigation volume;
- that the historical exploratory result was preregistered;
- that the clean historical model is an independent replication;
- that the 2024-2025 extension was conceived without knowledge of the
  2022-2023 result;
- that a later result erases the failed or indeterminate original held-out
  test.

---

## 13. Reporting commitment

The 2022-2025 extension will remain reportable regardless of whether its
association estimate is:

- positive;
- negative;
- near zero;
- statistically significant;
- statistically non-significant;
- highly imprecise;
- temporally unstable;
- sensitive to individual wells or years.

No result category will cause the frozen measurement bridge, flooding
baseline, 10-km support, groundwater outcome, antecedent definition, or prior
2022-2023 result to be rewritten.

---

## Stage-1 conclusion

The project contains multiple evidence layers with different degrees of
independence.

The historical association is exploratory in origin.

The clean historical model is a frozen but historically informed re-analysis.

The 2022-2023 analysis is the genuine held-out association test and did not
corroborate the historical relationship.

The 2024-2025 observations are a later extension developed after that result
was known, while retaining outcome-independent measurement and data-processing
rules.

The next analysis must preserve these distinctions permanently.
